Planets of the Solar System

The solar system consists of eight planets, each with its own unique characteristics and features. These planets include Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. Each planet has its own orbit around the Sun, and they all contribute to the dynamic and complex nature of our solar system. The planets are classified into two main categories: the terrestrial planets (inner planets) and the giant planets (outer planets). The inner planets are rocky and have solid surfaces, while the outer planets are gas giants with swirling gases above a core. The solar system is part of the Milky Way galaxy, and our planet, Earth, is the only one known to support life.
